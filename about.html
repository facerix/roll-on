About Roll On

This app was built using standard web technologies by Rylee Corradini. It's a Progressive Web App, meaning you can install it directly to your tablet/phone screen/desktop like a regular app, and it will behave more or less the same as one, and save its data in your device storage.

Roll On is a driving game where you control an 18-wheeler truck just trying to survive, getting your cargo from point A to point B, avoiding whatever obstacles the road throws at you.

Built as a Progressive Web App, it installs like a native app and persists game data in your device storage.
